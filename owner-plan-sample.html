Your Property Improvement Plan

These are recommended improvements to achieve the results sent to you in your property appraisal.

Property
SAMPLE - 12 Example Street, Culburra Beach NSW 2540
Recommendations
13 across 7 areas

Overall property

  1. 1Install a dishwasher, washing machine and tumble dryer. A heat pump dryer is quieter and more energy efficient.
  2. 2Supply an espresso machine. A Nespresso compatible pod machine is the simplest option for guests.
  3. 3Install smoke alarms that meet AS 3786 and provide a fire safety certificate before the property goes live.

The kitchen is a strong feature. Adding the items above brings it up to guest expectations.

Exterior

  1. 1Install keyless entry or a combination lock box at the front door. Please avoid systems that need a new code for each guest or an app.
  2. 2Supply a quality BBQ sized for the guest capacity (no cheap flat plate models), with cleaning tools and two gas cylinders.

Garden

  1. 1Fully enclose the garden with no gaps in the fencing (or add a fenced dog run) so the property can be marketed as pet friendly.

Most of the fence is sound. The gap is along the rear boundary near the shed.

Living area 1

  1. 1Install a large smart TV (75 inch where space allows), or add a Google TV device to an existing TV.
  2. 2Add lamps for reading and evening lighting. Dimmer switches also help create atmosphere.

Bedroom 2

  1. 1Upgrade the double bed to a queen bed.
  2. 2Fit blackout blinds or curtains.

Bedroom 3

  1. 1Provide a fan and a heater, as the room has no air conditioning. Oil column heaters are a safe, low-cost option.

Bathroom 1

  1. 1Supply shampoo, conditioner, shower gel and hand soap in a pump dispenser.
  2. 2Supply a hair dryer.
